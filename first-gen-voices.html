Featured note / First-Gen Voices

What I Learned Making First-Gen Voices

I started a podcast because first-generation students were already trading useful stories privately. These are the lessons I am taking from making 28 episodes.

Why I made itI wanted a thoughtful place for first-generation student stories without trying to speak for every first-generation student.
At a glance28 episodes
4,500+ downloads
Started in 2024

I started First-Gen Voices in 2024 to interview first-generation college students and make room for stories that are often traded privately.

I am the founder and producer, so I handle the recording, editing, cover art, and release scheduling. The podcast has reached 28 episodes and more than 4,500 downloads.

Creative work with a clear purpose

The project has given me a practical way to combine storytelling, audio production, visual work, and consistent publishing. Each episode is an opportunity to listen carefully and make a complex experience easier for another student to understand.

What I am taking forward

First-Gen Voices keeps reminding me that useful communication starts with curiosity. The work is not only the conversation itself; it is also the care taken to prepare, edit, package, and release it.

I am still figuring out what would help another student creator most, but I know that thoughtful questions, clear editing, and a dependable process are worth keeping.

Thanks for reading. I hope one of these small lessons helps you start.
